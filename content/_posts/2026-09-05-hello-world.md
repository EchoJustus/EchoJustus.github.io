# Hello, world

This is a starter post. Posts live in `content/_posts/`, are named
`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, and are not numbered: they sort by date rather than by
sidebar position, and they do not appear in the knowledge-base tree.

The date in the filename is for humans and for sorting the directory listing;
the date the site actually sorts on is the `date:` field in the front matter
above. Both are written here so they agree.

A post can be filed under a category by putting it in a subdirectory —
`content/_posts/notes/2026-09-05-hello-world.md` files it under *notes*. A post
with no subdirectory gets the category configured as `:content :category-text`
in `site.edn`, which is *Notes*.

Posts take language suffixes exactly like articles do:
`2026-09-05-hello-world.zh-Hans.md` is the same post, not a second one.
